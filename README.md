DESCRIPTION OF PROJECT:
Asmogtheus is a post Devourer of Gods optional superboss and is supposed to be stupid difficult
with a fun cheesy way to one shot it. Asmogtheus (Pronounce the "Asmog" like the "osmog" in Cosmog from Pokemon, the Theus pronounced "eus" like Odysseus)

OPTIONAL superboss
4 million HP (Classic Mode), scales based on difficulty (10 Million in Revengeance)
Constantly aggros onto the player with the highest defense in a 1200 tile radius.
Splits into 2 Divine Servants (Like Desert Nuisance) when it reaches 50% HP.
99% Damage reduction on spawn, goes down as it takes more damage, at 25% HP, it has 25% damage reduction. 
Enrages when it hits 25% HP, it roars, making it heal to 75% HP, and making all players around it generate Adrenaline 50% faster.
When enraged, it functions like Astrum Deus, flying around instead of burrowing.
Turns Underworld (Hell) into Tundra Biome until defeated. Makes Destroyer spawn whenever the player enters Space Biome until defeated. (If spawned and left undefeated)
Cheese: If hit by the rogue weapon "Exorcism" with the Flawless reforge, it dies immediately.

Most of the code I added is useless, because I made it with AI a month ago and reworked the idea (Was originally supposed to be a pre-Yharon fight that locked Auric Ore and dropped Arc of the Cosmos)

Messages sent in chat
On Spawn: "The Heavens tremble, The Underworld freezes in fear" and then "Asmogtheus the Ruinous Devourer has awoken!"
On 50% HP: "The Divine Servants have awoken!"
On Death: Normal "Asmogtheus' influence on {World Name} has faded." Exorcism Death: "The power of the Heavens has purged The Ruinous Devourer."

If Asmogtheus is defeated by Exorcism, the fight ends immediately, and it drops the Asmogtheus relic, and the Ruinous Devourer treasure bag.
